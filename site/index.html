nimbo

Reliable mirror backups from Linux to Google Drive, with a terminal dashboard.

nimbo is an open-source desktop tool. It runs on your own computer and copies the folders you choose to a folder in your own Google Drive, safely and resumably. There is no nimbo server: your files and credentials never pass through the developers.

Status: in development. Source code and documentation will be published in this repository.
